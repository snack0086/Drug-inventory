# 🖥️ Module 10: Multi-Role Persona-Tailored Dashboards

> **Differentiated User Experiences:** Four specialized, high-productivity dashboards engineered to deliver zero cognitive clutter and role-specific operational intelligence.

---

## 🎯 1. Module Objective

A critical architectural failure of generic ERP systems is exposing every user to the same generic data grid. **Our system** establishes four strictly segregated, persona-driven frontend interfaces. Each user views only the metrics, geographic scope, and action controls required to answer their core operational question.

### 🏛️ Dashboard A: State Government Officer (State-Level Macro View)
* **Core Question Answered:** *"What is happening across the entire state supply chain, and where are systemic risks emerging?"*
* **Key Visual Panels:**
  1. **Statewide Supply KPI Bar:** Total Active Drugs, Total State Stock Valuation, Stockout Rate (%), Expired Stock Wastage ($), Active Transit Shipments.
  2. **Interactive Geographic Risk Map:** Color-coded hospital markers (Green = Normal, Yellow = Elevated, Red = Critical Risk Score $>80$). Clicking reveals facility burn rate and stock runway.
  3. **High-Risk Facilities & Critical Stockout Watchlist:** Prioritized list of peripheral hospitals with $< 5$ days of vital stock remaining.
  4. **Redistribution Approval Center:** One-click review panel displaying transfer recommendations generated by **Our Intelligent Redistribution Engine (Module 08)** and **Near-Expiry Rescue Engine (Module 09)**.
  5. **Vendor Performance Matrix:** On-time fulfillment scores, batch rejection rates, and contract SLA compliance.

---

### 🏥 Dashboard B: Hospital Pharmacist (Facility-Specific View)
* **Core Question Answered:** *"What do we have on hand, what are we consuming right now, and what will run out next week?"*
* **Key Visual Panels:**
  1. **Hospital Shelf Runway Card:** Total drug formulations on hand, days of inventory remaining, daily burn rate.
  2. **Batch Expiry Timeline & FEFO Queue:** Chronological calendar highlighting batches expiring in $\le 30, 60, 90$ days.
  3. **Point-of-Care Consumption Logger:** Rapid dispensing input modal with barcode scanning verification.
  4. **Inbound & Outbound Transfer Gate:**
     * Inbound: Accept and verify incoming transfer shipments from warehouses or peer hospitals.
     * Outbound: Picking and packing checklist for approved lateral transfers to peer hospitals.
  5. **Emergency Requisition Assistant:** Auto-computed order quantities ($Q_{required}$) based on historical rolling 14-day consumption.

---

### 🏬 Dashboard C: Warehouse Manager (Logistics & Physical Distribution)
* **Core Question Answered:** *"What inventory needs to move, to which hospital, when, and on what vehicle?"*
* **Key Visual Panels:**
  1. **Warehouse Floor Capacity & Bin Stock:** Physical storage breakdown across Ambient, Cold-Chain ($2^\circ\text{C}-8^\circ\text{C}$), and Secure Lockbox zones.
  2. **Inward Receiving & QC Dock:** Inbound vendor shipments awaiting lab inspection, temperature logger checks, and acceptance sign-offs.
  3. **Picking, Packing & Dispatch Queue:** Daily dispatch orders sorted by vehicle routes, target hospitals, and urgency.
  4. **In-Transit Delay Alert Board:** Real-time carrier tracking highlighting delayed shipments exceeding SLA thresholds.
  5. **Cold-Chain Alert Stream:** Live IoT telemetry warnings for temperature excursions in storage or transit.

---

### 🏭 Dashboard D: Procurement Vendor (Supply Order Portal)
* **Core Question Answered:** *"What purchase orders must I supply, and what is the status of my recent deliveries?"*
* **Key Visual Panels:**
  1. **Purchase Order Inbox:** New, acknowledged, and ongoing PO contracts with line-item volume breakdowns.
  2. **Shipment Dispatch Creator:** Form to input dispatch lot numbers, manufacturing dates, lab assay certificates, and courier tracking details.
  3. **Quality & Rejection Feedback Center:** Instant inspection reports from warehouse receiving docks with detailed rationale for accepted/rejected batches.
  4. **Performance Scorecard:** Real-time visibility into the vendor's own On-Time Delivery % and Fulfillment Accuracy rating.
